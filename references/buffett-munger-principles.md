# Buffett–Munger Active-Investing Principles

Use this framework only inside the active-investing exception path. Bogle governs the portfolio default; Buffett and Munger govern whether an individual company earns an exception.

The principles below are synthesized from the source roots in [source-registry.md](source-registry.md). They are not quotations and must be applied to evidence, not invoked as slogans.

## Principle cards

All `BM` cards have `active-exception` authority and cannot override the Bogle default or a higher boundary.

| ID | Principle | Scope and required question | Hard-stop signal | Prohibited inference | Source roots |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BM-01` | Circle of competence | Comprehension: can the investor explain the business, industry, variables, and failure modes? | Critical economics depend on variables the investor cannot evaluate | Familiarity with the product proves business understanding | `BRK-LTR`, `PCA` |
| `BM-02` | Business ownership | Thesis: would the business remain attractive without market quotations for several years? | Thesis depends mainly on momentum, catalysts, or resale | A long holding intention proves owner orientation | `BRK-OM`, `BRK-LTR` |
| `BM-03` | Durable economics | Business quality: why do customers choose it, and what protects the economics? | Advantage is asserted but not evidenced, or change is too rapid to judge | Revenue growth proves a durable moat | `BRK-LTR`, `BRK-AR` |
| `BM-04` | Owner economics | Cash conversion: do profits become distributable cash after maintenance needs? | No credible path from growth to per-share owner earnings | Accounting profit equals owner earnings | `BRK-OM`, `BRK-LTR` |
| `BM-05` | Management and incentives | Stewardship: are managers candid and rational with owner capital? | Governance blocks accountability, disclosure is promotional, or dilution is ignored | Charismatic management proves aligned incentives | `BRK-OM`, `PCA`, `DJCO` |
| `BM-06` | Resilience | Permanent loss: can the company survive stress without forced financing? | Fragile funding, hidden leverage, severe dependence, or existential regulation | Current cash alone proves resilience | `BRK-OM`, `BRK-LTR` |
| `BM-07` | Value and margin of safety | Valuation: is a conservative intrinsic-value range supportable? | Price cannot be compared with a defensible value range | A low multiple or price decline proves margin of safety | `BRK-OM`, `BRK-LTR` |
| `BM-08` | Opportunity cost | Capital eligibility: is this preferable to the passive baseline after risk and effort? | The case only looks attractive in isolation | A qualified company must receive capital | `BRK-LTR`, `PCA` |
| `BM-09` | Inversion | Failure analysis: what combination could make the thesis fail? | A plausible permanent-loss path has no adequate protection | Naming a risk means it has been controlled | `PCA`, `DJCO` |
| `BM-10` | Temperament and patience | Implementation: can the investor follow the thesis without price-led reactions? | Position size or personal liquidity makes rational holding unlikely | Confidence proves behavioral capacity | `BRK-LTR`, `PCA` |

## Application rules

1. Evaluate each principle only in the stage that consumes it, and record `Pass`, `Unclear`, `Fail`, or a valid `Not Applicable` with evidence and source. `BM-01` maps to CR-2; `BM-02` to `BM-04` to CR-3; `BM-05` to CR-4; `BM-06` to CR-5; `BM-07` to CR-6; and `BM-09` to CR-7. Record `BM-08` in Stage 1 CE-4 and use `BM-10` when setting caps and implementing a holding; do not rescore either as company quality.
2. `Unclear` is not half a pass. Each principle inherits the criticality and aggregation rule of the stage or gate that consumes it; [active-investment-workflow.md](active-investment-workflow.md) is the single source of those rules. `Not Applicable` is permitted only for a genuinely irrelevant test inside a `Required` company-research gate, with a written reason. It is never valid for a `Critical` gate, Stage 1, or a material test merely lacking evidence. An unresolved material principle blocks `Qualified exception` through its gate.
3. Do not average a governance failure against revenue growth or a valuation failure against product quality.
4. Use a range for intrinsic value. If loss-making economics make owner earnings highly speculative, valuation remains `Unclear` even when growth is strong.
5. Evaluate per-share outcomes. Capital raised, acquisitions, options, restricted shares, and convertible instruments can make company growth diverge from owner value.
6. Treat the cost basis as irrelevant to prospective value except for taxes and implementation constraints.

## Boundary of the framework

This method does not claim that Buffett or Munger would buy, hold, or sell a security. It tests whether the user's evidence satisfies a disciplined interpretation of their decision principles. It must never produce imagined endorsements or personality-based predictions.
